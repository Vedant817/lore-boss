## Implementation assessment and objections to my own claims

The safeguards are substantive. `src/lore/service.ts:33–46` checks schema, distinct referenced IDs, author presence, quote substring and blocked text; `:60–75` snapshots the buffer/session generation and discards superseded work. `src/app.ts:58–75` scopes evidence/review to the active session. `src/runtime.ts:153–180` checks source/channel/session and claims event IDs before processing. Combat arithmetic is separated into a pure reducer, while scheduling remains wall-clock runtime behavior. Replay is explicitly selected and forced/labeled; a recording alone is not deterministic whole-session scheduling.

Objections to the positive assessment: IDs and verbatim substrings do **not** validate the semantic truth of catchphrases, moments or rivalries, and moderator edits are not re-proven quotes. The public copy now acknowledges that. Prompt instructions and an email/phone/blocklist filter are not a general privacy or prompt-injection guarantee. Durable event claims also should not be described as an audited transaction spanning every combat side effect: I did not fault-inject database failures or certify crash recovery. The unit suite passes on this checkout, but browser totals, hosted CI, OBS and live integrations were not independently rerun here.

Objections to the negative assessment: I might underweight how entertaining recognition of an actual in-joke can be; the solo capture cannot decide that either way. Potential Impact asks about potential, not proven traction, so I have not scored it as zero for lacking research. I might undervalue the text-only overlay's practical suitability over gameplay; absent portraits are not a functionality defect. The video's substantial Codex evidence answers a formal judging interest, and the clear safety disclosures should not be mistaken for lack of implementation. Conversely, volume of tests and provenance should not automatically earn full implementation or design marks. This is a bounded judge review, not a comprehensive security/load/accessibility audit.
